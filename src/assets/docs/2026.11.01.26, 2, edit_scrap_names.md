Scrap names in the Scraps list can now be edited in place.

Double-click a name, or select a row and press F2. Enter applies the name, Escape cancels and leaves the dialog open, and clicking away applies a valid name. A blank name or one already used by another scrap is refused, and the row stays put. Double-clicking the rest of the row still loads that scrap.
