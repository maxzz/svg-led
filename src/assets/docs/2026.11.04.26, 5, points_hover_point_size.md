# Direct selection cursor margin
_Exported on 11/4/2026 at 18:17:18 PST from Cursor (3.28.7)_

---

**User**

In Direct Selection Mode, when the cursor hovers over a point, the point grows larger—which is exactly the desired behavior. 
The issue, however, is that the cursor must be positioned precisely at the center of the handle representing the point. 
It would be better if there were a small margin—at least one pixel in every direction—within which the system would detect that the cursor is over the point.

---

**Cursor**

The hit-testing is DOM-based: `resolveTarget` looks for `data-hit` on the element under the pointer. The visible anchor squares are only 4px and the handle circles have a 3px radius, so that is the exact area that counts as a hit. I'll add invisible, slightly larger hit areas with the same `data-*` attributes, leaving the visible sizes unchanged.
