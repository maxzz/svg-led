# Direct selection cursor issue
_Exported on 11/3/2026 at 17:31:29 PST from Cursor (3.27.22)_

---

**User**

Several cursors were implemented for the Direct Selection mode, but for some reason, they do not appear—only the standard cursor is shown. This needs to be fixed.

---

**Cursor**

I'll look through how Direct Selection cursors are defined and applied, then trace why the standard cursor still shows.

Direct Selection leaves the canvas on the default arrow. The move and pointer cursors are only set on the point widgets, so they never take over the tool. I’ll put the tool’s cursors on the canvas itself, the same way Selection and the Pen do.

Direct Selection now uses its own cursors on the canvas. The tool was leaving the canvas on the system arrow, and the move and pointer cursors were only set on the point widgets, so they never replaced that arrow.

The canvas cursor now follows the pointer:

- **Empty canvas:** a white arrow.
- **Over an object that is not selected:** the arrow plus a small square.
- **Over a selected object, point, or handle, and while dragging:** the arrow plus a move icon.
- **Ctrl over a segment:** a plus. **Ctrl over a point:** a minus.
- **Alt over a point:** the convert mark.

I checked this in the running app: the canvas picks up the white arrow, and it switches to the hover, move, delete, and convert cursors.
