- Each ruler has short ticks, a slightly longer tick halfway between numbers when there's room, and long ticks with a number.
- The numbers are in viewBox units, at 8px. Ticks and numbers are a pale gray, so they don't draw attention.
- The left ruler's numbers are rotated to read bottom to top, like a ruler turned on its side.

**How density adapts**
- Numbered ticks are always at least 48px apart on screen, and step in 1, 2 or 5 times a power of ten. Unnumbered ticks are at least 5px apart.
- On a 24×24 icon: at 1240% zoom there's a number every 5 units, at 794% every 10, and at 406% every 20.
- A very large viewBox works the same way, giving numbers like 0, 500, 1000 instead of a crowded scale. Very high zoom gives fractional steps like 0.5.
- Only the part of each ruler that's on screen is generated, so zooming far in doesn't produce thousands of ticks.

The change is in `rulerTicks` in `5-rulers/1-ruler-ticks.ts`. It adds a long numbered tick at the far edge of the viewBox, or turns the existing tick there into one. Some details:

- **Final value already on the step:** if the end lands on a step anyway (24 with a step of 2), nothing is duplicated.
- **Fractional sizes:** a viewBox like 24.375 wide shows "24.375", up to 3 decimal places.
- **Overlapping numbers:** if the final number would overlap the number just before it, the earlier number is dropped and the final one stays. The tick itself stays, just without its number. With a width of 20.5 and a step of 5, the 20 gives way to 20.5. The overlap check uses the actual label lengths, so "20" and "24" can still sit 16px apart (406% zoom) without hiding the 20.
- **Off screen:** if the far end is panned off screen, it isn't drawn.

The start of the ruler hasn't changed. With a viewBox like `3 3 24 24` and a step of 5, the first number is 5, not 3. I can add the same treatment for the starting value if you want.
