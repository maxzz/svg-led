**User**

When the application loads for the first time, it initially appears in the top-left corner at a small size, before the full application—with its canvas—displays in the center at the correct scale. 
This brief transition during startup creates an unpleasant experience for the user and needs to be fixed. Ideally, the canvas should fade in smoothly when the application first loads.
